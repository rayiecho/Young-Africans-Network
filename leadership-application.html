Lead the Movement.
Serve Young Africans Network.

YAN is strengthening its leadership and management structure as we grow our programs, partnerships and community impact across Africa. We're inviting committed young Africans to step up — no formal title required, just commitment, integrity and the willingness to learn.

Status: Open Deadline: To be communicated Type: Volunteer Leadership
Start Your Application ↓
Before You Apply

What We're Looking For

We're looking for individuals who demonstrate commitment, integrity, responsibility, leadership potential, teamwork, initiative and a willingness to learn. Formal professional leadership experience is not mandatory — relevant leadership, volunteering, academic, community or project experience will be considered where you can show strong potential and commitment.

01
Potential Over Titles
You don't need a formal leadership title. We care about what you've done, what you've learned, and what you're capable of contributing.
02
Service-Driven
YAN is youth-led and volunteer-driven. Leadership here is built on service, responsibility and accountability — not paid employment.
03
Pan-African Impact
Help shape programs, partnerships and community impact reaching young people across Africa — starting from wherever you are.
Important

These are voluntary, unpaid positions and do not create an employer-employee relationship with YAN. Due to the number of applications expected, only shortlisted applicants will be contacted for the next stage — which may include an interview or leadership conversation before final appointments are made.
