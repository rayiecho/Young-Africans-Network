Our Partners

YAN works with mission-aligned organizations to widen the doors open to our members. Meet the partners helping us deliver on that promise.

Star9 Freelancer Ltd

Star9 Freelancer Ltd is a freelancing and digital skills training company empowering African youth and women with in-demand skills, remote work opportunities, and pathways to study and work abroad — freelancing with heart and skill.

Through this partnership, Star9 delivers hands-on digital skills and freelancing training directly to YAN members, at no cost to members, with Star9 covering the training investment.

What This Partnership Brings YAN Members

💻

Digital Skills Training

Practical, guided training in in-demand freelance skills — from the basics to advanced techniques.

🌍

Remote Work Access

Pathways into real remote work opportunities with international clients.

🎓

Study & Work Abroad Support

Guidance and access to study-abroad and work-abroad opportunities for qualifying members.
